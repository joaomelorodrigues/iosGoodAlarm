# Good Alarm Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Good Alarm does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your alarms, settings and imported sounds are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad. They are removed when you delete the app.
- **Music library, Photos and Files.** When you pick a song, video or file, the app copies only its audio into its own storage so it can play it as an alarm. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Alarms and notifications.** The app uses Apple's AlarmKit and local notifications to ring. These are handled by iOS on your device.

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published on this page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/joaomelorodrigues/iosGoodAlarm/issues
