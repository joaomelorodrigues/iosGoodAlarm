## Description (4000)

Wake up to the song you actually want, at the volume you choose, fading in as gently as you like.

Good Alarm gives iPhone and iPad the alarm features people have asked for for years:

ANY SOUND YOU LIKE
• Songs you own from your music library
• The soundtrack of any video in your Photos
• Any audio or video file from Files: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and more
• Start the song at the chorus instead of the quiet intro
• Four built-in tones if you just want something simple

VOLUME AND FADE, PER ALARM
• Set each alarm's volume on its own
• Gradually increase volume, from 5 seconds to 5 minutes
• Preview exactly how it will sound before you save

SNOOZE YOUR WAY
• Choose a snooze length from 1 to 30 minutes, or turn snooze off

VIBRATION (IPHONE)
• Vibrate-only alarms that make no sound, so you don't wake anyone else
• Vibration patterns in full-song mode: Standard, Heartbeat, Rapid and Escalating

SMARTER SCHEDULING
• Skip just the next ring of a repeating alarm, or dismiss a one-time alarm early
• One-time alarms switch themselves off, or delete themselves, after ringing
• Quick alarms in 10, 20, 30 or 60 minutes for naps
• "Alarm set for 7 h 32 min from now", so you know you got it right

RELIABLE
• Built on Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms ring through silent mode and Focus, even when the app is closed
• Test any alarm in 10 seconds to hear exactly what will wake you
• Snooze countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

FULL-SONG MODE (OPTIONAL)
Turn it on in Settings and leave Good Alarm in the app switcher. It then plays your whole song, sets the phone to your chosen volume, and fades in over as long as you like. If you sleep through it, a system safety alarm still rings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no account, no tracking. Your alarms and sounds never leave your device.

Note: Apple Music streaming songs are protected by Apple and can't be used by any alarm app. Songs you bought or synced, and your own files, work.

## App Review notes

Good Alarm is an alarm clock built on AlarmKit. No account or login is needed.

To review:
1. Allow alarms when asked on first launch.
2. Open any alarm and tap "Test system alarm in 10 seconds", then lock the device. The alarm rings with the selected sound.
3. Sounds can be imported from the music library, from a video in Photos, or from Files (Edit alarm › Sound).

Background audio: "Full-song mode" is off by default (Settings › Full-song mode). When the user turns it on, Good Alarm keeps its audio session active while an alarm is due within 24 hours, so that it can play the user's whole song audibly, at the chosen volume and with the chosen fade, at alarm time. The audio session stops when no alarm is due. To try it: turn it on, add a quick alarm with "Quick alarm › In 10 minutes", go to the Home Screen, and the song plays at alarm time.
